    How to play

    1. Select 5 tiles anywhere on the board. Click a selected tile again to deselect it.
    2. Replace all selected tiles with a letter strictly between the smallest and biggest selected letters in the alphabet. The endpoints are excluded. For example, selecting D, F, H, I, M gives you the choices E, F, G, H, I, J, K, or L. Choose from the menu or type a letter.
    3. Words of 4–5 letters are found automatically horizontally, left to right →, and vertically, top to bottom ↓. Letters must be consecutive in one row or column. Each unique word earns one point per letter, once per game.

    Every board starts without words. There’s no timer. When you’re out of ideas, press End game to see your score.
